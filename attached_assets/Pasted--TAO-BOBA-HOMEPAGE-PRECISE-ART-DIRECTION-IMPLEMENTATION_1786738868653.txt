# TAO BOBA HOMEPAGE — PRECISE ART DIRECTION / IMPLEMENTATION SPEC

## READ THIS BEFORE CHANGING ANYTHING

Do not “interpret” this brief into another design concept.

Do not improvise a premium café website.

Do not add decorative elements because an area appears empty.

For this iteration, follow the **layout measurements, positioning, hierarchy and spacing below as closely as possible**.

### Primary creative reference

**Andtea.com**

This is the reference the CLIENT gave us.

Andtea should guide:

* product presentation
* drink choreography
* scrolling behavior
* motion
* visual richness
* interaction between product, type and imagery

### Spatial/layout reference

**Velora**
https://velora-template.framer.website/

Velora should guide:

* hero composition
* image placement
* asymmetric spacing
* typography positioning
* relationship between large image and smaller text
* calmness of the UI

### Other aesthetic references

EN TEA, SABOE, HIGASHIYA, Sakurai, Kettl and Rishi Tea are references for:

* elegance
* rhythm
* photography treatment
* editorial pacing
* spacing
* visual hierarchy

Do NOT turn those references into extreme emptiness.

---

# CENTRAL PRINCIPLE

## VISUALLY RICH, STRUCTURALLY MINIMAL.

We want:

* lots of beautiful Tao imagery
* very little visible UI
* varied compositions
* photography that changes in scale and position
* elegant typography
* carefully controlled motion
* substantial breathing room

We do NOT want:

* barren whitespace with a single cup
* obvious cards
* conventional website sections
* 50/50 hero
* product grids
* generic “luxury café” styling

# HERO PHOTO CONTENT

Use one of Tao Boba's strongest photographs.

A photograph with multiple drinks is acceptable **if the photograph itself is art-directed beautifully**.

The mistake is NOT “multiple drinks are forbidden.”

The mistake is using an image that looks like:

> five ecommerce products lined up to show the menu.

Prefer imagery where the drinks form a composition.

Possible:

* two or three drinks with interesting crops
* drinks at different heights
* dramatic close crop
* hand entering frame
* table composition
* preparation scene with finished drinks
* product + ingredient environment

The photograph needs visual atmosphere.

---

# HERO IMAGE CROP

Avoid showing every cup perfectly from top to bottom.

Crop deliberately.

At least one object may:

* approach image edge
* partially leave the frame
* overlap another object visually
* be significantly larger than another

The image should feel editorial, not informational.

---

# CRITICAL MOTION RULE

Once the drink reaches the final position:

## IT BECOMES COMPLETELY STILL.

No idle float.

No bobbing.

No rotating.

No looping movement.

No “alive” animation.

The elegance comes from motion followed by stillness.

Reverse scrolling should mirror the interaction naturally.

# FINAL VISUAL TEST AT 1440 × 900

Before considering the design complete, capture the homepage at 1440 × 900 and review the first viewport.

Ask:

### Can I identify one dominant image immediately?

Must be YES.

### Does the first viewport contain more than 3 strong focal points?

If YES, simplify.

### Does the image feel intentionally positioned rather than centered automatically?

Must be YES.

### Does this look like Velora with Tao photos copied in?

If YES, it is too literal.

### Does this look like Andtea copied with Tao branding?

If YES, it is too literal.

### Does it feel like Tao's own art-directed world while clearly understanding why the client referenced Andtea?

That is the target.
