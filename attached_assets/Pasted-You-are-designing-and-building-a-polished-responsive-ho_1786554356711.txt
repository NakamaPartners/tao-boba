Objective
Create a spectacular, presentation ready homepage that makes the brand feel more engaging, memorable, desirable, and commercially effective.
This is a homepage concept intended to demonstrate what the brand could become through stronger creative direction and digital execution. Concentrate the design effort on one exceptionally resolved homepage. Do not quietly expand the project into a complete multi page rebuild.
The result should feel intentionally art directed, emotionally compelling, and unmistakably connected to this specific brand. It must not look like a generic template, a typical startup website, or an unrelated redesign imposed on the business.
Build the working experience directly in the current Replit project. Do not stop after presenting a plan or design description.
Source hierarchy
Use the following hierarchy whenever making a decision:
The Notion creative direction is the primary source of truth.
The existing website provides validated business information, services, products, terminology, and available brand assets.
Instagram provides additional validated photography, content, tone, atmosphere, customer moments, and brand behavior.
External references may improve design craft, interaction quality, composition, usability, and technical execution only.
The Notion direction defines the creative corridor. Stay inside it.
Do not introduce colors, fonts, visual motifs, photography treatments, messaging angles, or aesthetic concepts that contradict or dilute the Notion direction.
External inspiration may help answer questions such as how to create better pacing, responsive behavior, transitions, hierarchy, navigation, and interaction. It must not establish a new creative direction.
Never reproduce another brand’s identity, artwork, copy, or distinctive layout.
Research before designing
Before writing code:
Read the entire Notion page, including every section, embedded image, caption, reference, and linked asset that is accessible.
Explore the existing website page by page and understand what the company offers, how it describes itself, what actions customers can take, and which information is factual.
Review the Instagram carefully to understand the real visual language, products, people, environment, customer experience, and tone.
Identify the strongest existing brand assets and the weaknesses the new homepage should correct.
Translate the Notion direction into explicit design rules covering typography, color hierarchy, photography, spacing, composition, corners, motion, texture, and interaction.
Resolve every design decision against those rules before implementing it.
If a source cannot be accessed, do not pretend that it was reviewed. Continue only with information that can be verified from the accessible sources.
If the sources conflict, follow the Notion creative direction for visual decisions and the existing website for factual business information.
Creative standard
Treat the homepage as a connected visual system, not a collection of attractive sections.
Every choice should have a reason grounded in the Notion concept:
Typography must follow the documented type direction and hierarchy.
Color must follow the documented palette and intended emotional effect.
Photography must follow the documented mood, framing, warmth, intimacy, and treatment.
Layout must express the documented approach to space, rhythm, scale, and composition.
Corners, containers, borders, and interface elements must reflect the established visual character.
Motion must reinforce the brand’s emotional tone rather than functioning as decoration.
Every section should feel like part of the same world.
The result should feel highly specific, human, editorial, and considered. Avoid generic interface patterns unless the brand genuinely requires them.
Do not use default SaaS cards, excessive pills, arbitrary gradients, glass effects, random blobs, generic icon collections, stock layout formulas, or trendy effects that have no basis in the Notion direction.
Homepage experience
Create a complete homepage journey with a strong narrative and clear commercial purpose.
Determine the most appropriate sequence from the validated source material. The page should generally accomplish the following:
Establish the brand’s atmosphere and proposition immediately.
Introduce the most important offering without overwhelming the visitor.
Build desire through authentic imagery, details, products, rituals, place, people, or process.
Communicate what makes the brand distinctive.
Provide useful proof or context where validated content exists.
Guide the visitor toward the most important real customer action.
End with a memorable and purposeful closing moment.
Do not add sections simply to make the page longer. Each section must contribute to the narrative, clarify the offer, build trust, create desire, or support conversion.
Use deliberate changes in scale, density, alignment, image proportion, and whitespace to create pacing. Avoid a repetitive stack of equally sized rectangular sections.
Content integrity
Use only factual claims, names, product descriptions, services, locations, quotations, testimonials, awards, statistics, contact details, operating information, and calls to action that can be validated through the supplied sources.
Do not invent brand statements, customer promises, testimonials, product names, prices, achievements, statistics, or company history.
You may carefully edit verified copy for clarity and digital presentation, but you must preserve its meaning.
If essential copy is unavailable, use a concise structural label such as “Content required” during development. Do not disguise invented content as final brand copy.
Do not use em dashes or en dashes in any customer facing copy. Rewrite the sentence naturally using commas, periods, or separate sentences.
Image and asset rules
Use authentic assets from the existing website, Instagram, or Notion whenever technically accessible and appropriate.
Do not use AI generated photography or illustrations.
Do not use the same photograph more than once anywhere on the homepage, including alternate crops, backgrounds, cards, or mobile variants.
Do not repeat an image simply to fill space. If there are not enough suitable assets, solve the section through typography, color, spacing, graphic composition, or documented brand elements.
Preserve image quality and natural proportions. Do not stretch, distort, overprocess, or apply treatments that conflict with the Notion photography direction.
Do not use unrelated stock imagery or copy third party brand assets. External sources are for creative reference, not for taking unlicensed imagery.
Interaction and motion
Use motion selectively to make the experience feel alive and intentional.
Motion should support the Notion direction and may include restrained image reveals, text sequencing, transitions, hover responses, scrolling behavior, or subtle compositional movement.
Avoid excessive parallax, constant floating, distracting cursor effects, long intro animations, or motion that delays access to content.
Respect reduced motion preferences.
Every interaction must have a clear purpose. It should improve orientation, hierarchy, discovery, feedback, or emotional impact.
Responsive and technical quality
The homepage must be fully functional and carefully designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
Do not treat mobile as a compressed desktop layout. Recompose sections where necessary while preserving the concept, hierarchy, and emotional effect.
Ensure:
Semantic structure and accessible navigation.
Keyboard usability.
Visible focus states.
Appropriate contrast.
Meaningful alternative text.
Responsive typography.
Stable layouts without overflow.
Optimized images and sensible loading behavior.
Smooth performance.
No broken links, missing assets, console errors, or unfinished placeholder components.
Real buttons and links use validated destinations whenever available.
Decorative controls do not pretend to be functional.
Use the project’s existing framework and components intelligently. Do not replace a sound technical foundation without a clear reason.
Implementation process
Work in this order:
Audit all supplied sources.
Summarize the brand, audience, central creative tension, commercial goal, and Notion corridor in concise internal notes.
Define the homepage narrative and section sequence.
Establish reusable design tokens for color, typography, spacing, radii, motion, and layout.
Build the page as a coherent component system.
Add authentic content and unique imagery.
Refine responsive behavior.
Test interactions, links, accessibility, and performance.
Conduct a final visual consistency review.
Continue refining until the page feels presentation ready.
Do not ask me to choose between several generic visual directions. The direction has already been established in Notion. Interpret it intelligently and execute it decisively.
Final quality gate
Before considering the work complete, verify all of the following:
The homepage clearly follows the Notion creative direction.
No external influence has pushed the design outside that corridor.
The visual system feels specific to this brand.
Typography, color, imagery, spacing, corners, and motion reinforce one another.
The narrative is clear and commercially useful.
No unsupported claims or invented brand facts appear.
No AI generated imagery is used.
No photograph appears more than once.
No em dashes or en dashes appear in customer facing copy.
The design avoids generic templates and repetitive section structures.
Desktop, tablet, and mobile experiences all feel intentionally composed.
The finished build works without broken assets, overflow, dead interactions, or console errors.
Aim for a level of strategic clarity, visual coherence, specificity, rationale, and presentation craft that would score at least 85 out of 100 in a professional creative direction review.
The final result should make a viewer think: this could only belong to this brand.
